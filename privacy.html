1. Overview & Principles

softify Inc. ("softify", "we", "us", or "our") builds purpose-built, industry-specific business software. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding data entrusted to us.

Our Core Privacy Promise: We adhere strictly to data minimization principles. We only collect the minimal contact and usage data necessary to facilitate product evaluation, software demos, and system operations. We never monetize, sell, or rent customer information.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website (softify.example.com), demo walkthroughs, and related communication channels.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in three distinct categories:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Contact Inquiries: Name, professional email address, company name, subject line, and inquiry message submitted via our contact forms.
  • Demo Requests: Work email, company organization, estimated operational requirements, and product module preferences.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: Corporate email address for product engineering updates.

B. Automated Technical Telemetry

  • Server Log Data: Anonymized IP addresses, HTTP request timestamps, browser user agent strings, and HTTP response codes to maintain uptime and mitigate denial-of-service threats.
  • Functional Telemetry: Aggregated interaction metrics (e.g., CTA click events, product filter toggles) used exclusively to evaluate UX performance.

3. How We Use Data

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Scheduling and conducting personalized product walkthroughs tailored to your industry workflows.
  • Responding promptly to technical inquiries, partnership queries, and customer service requests.
  • Auditing server performance, security hardening, and protecting against unauthorized or automated bot traffic.
  • Transmitting relevant product release announcements (with one-click unsubscribe in every transmission).

4. Zero-Resale & Third-Party Policy

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not share customer contact records with ad exchanges, data brokers, or third-party behavioral advertising networks.

Data is only processed by essential infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting and transactional email transport) under strict confidentiality agreements and Data Processing Addendums (DPAs).

5. Security Safeguards

softify maintains robust organizational, physical, and technical safeguards:

  • Encryption: All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Data at rest is secured via AES-256 encryption.
  • Input Sanitization & Rate Limiting: All public endpoints incorporate layered rate-limiting and cryptographic token validation to prevent brute-force attacks and cross-site scripting (XSS).
  • Access Control: Administrative console access requires multi-factor session authentication and is restricted solely to authorized engineering personnel.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

softify takes an anti-bloat stance on web trackers. We utilize only essential and privacy-first functional mechanisms:

  • Essential Cookies: Ephemeral session tokens required for security authentication and CSRF protection.
  • Zero Cross-Site Trackers: We do not embed intrusive third-party retargeting pixels or ad-tracking scripts on our marketing pages.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the specific personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete records.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request immediate permanent deletion of your contact records.
  • Right to Object & Opt-Out: Opt out of communications or request processing limitations at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our compliance team at privacy@softify.io. Requests are fulfilled within 30 calendar days at zero cost.

8. International Transfers

softify infrastructure is hosted in enterprise-grade, ISO 27001-certified cloud facilities. When transferring data across international boundaries, we ensure adequate protection through Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and GDPR-aligned adequacy frameworks.

If you have questions, feedback, or concerns regarding our privacy practices or data governance, please reach out to our privacy office:
